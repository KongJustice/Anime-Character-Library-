The Art of Power: Where Legends Begin

      Before the domain expands, before the chant completes, before the heavens split or the curse breaks, there is a moment of silence. And in that silence, a hand moves.
  It is never just a hand. It is years of training. It is loss. It is obsession. It is a promise made to someone who is no longer there to hear it.
  Watch. Frieren flicks her fingers, almost lazily, and a thousand years of magic remembers her name. Gojo folds his hands, and infinity itself holds its breath.
  Sukuna grins and forms a shrine, and the world understands what malevolence truly means. A Demon Slayer tightens his grip, and that grip carries every comrade he could not save.
  These gestures are not for show. They are a language older than words. A contract between will and reality. Every seal, every spark, every sword grip is a declaration: I am here, and I will not bend.
  This is what you are watching. Not edits. Not effects. You are watching intention become form. You are watching ordinary fingers become vessels for impossible power. You are watching legends remember, for one more time, exactly who they are and why they refuse to fall.
                                This is the art. This is the power. This is anime.
